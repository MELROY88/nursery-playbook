Social media playbook · UAE nurseries · Calendar year 2027

Parents don't follow nurseries. They follow proof.

One plan any nursery can pick up and run: who we talk to, what we post, where, when, and how a "like" turns into a tour. Built for a small team, a sensible budget and a phone with a good camera.

…and yes, there are craft videos. Obviously.

8:00 · Morning Register

The plan on one page

Every post answers one of six questions

Parents choosing a nursery are asking these, out loud or at 2am. If a post answers none of them, it doesn't go up.

1Is my child safe here?
2Will my child be happy?
3Are the teachers kind and trained?
4Will my child actually learn?
5Will I know what's going on?
6Does it fit our family's life?

What we will do

  • Run 3 channels really well: Instagram, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business.
  • Post 6 times a week (weekends pre-scheduled), plus stories, from 6 content blocks.
  • Follow one 12-month calendar built on the UAE school year.
  • Reply to WhatsApp enquiries within 15 minutes in working hours.
  • Measure tours and enrolments, not just likes.

What we won't do

  • Chase every new platform or dance trend.
  • Buy followers or run "like & share to win" giveaways.
  • Post children whose parents haven't said yes in writing.
  • Make claims we can't show ("best nursery in Dubai").

8:05 · Good Morning Song

The goal, the promise and the ladder

Overall goal

Be the nursery local parents trust first, and recommend most.

Social media's job is to earn that trust before the first visit, then make the visit easy to book.

Positioning statement

"A safe, joyful nursery where every child is known by name, and every parent knows how their day went."

Make it your own: For families with children from __ months to __ years in your area, your nursery is the nursery that your one real difference, because the proof you can show.

Pick a difference you can film. "Small groups with one key teacher" is provable. "World-class education" is not.

What's shaping choices

  • Google Maps and reviews are the first shortlist.
  • WhatsApp is how families expect to talk to you.
  • Short real videos beat polished brochures.
  • Many languages at home; Arabic and English matter most.

What's changing

  • Child Digital Safety Law in force since January 2026.
  • Paid creators now need a Media Council permit.
  • 2026 is the UAE's Year of the Family, so family content fits the mood.
  • New nurseries keep opening, so standing out matters more.

Got several branches?

  • One main Instagram, but a Google profile and WhatsApp number per branch.
  • Tag the branch location on every post.
  • Each branch shows its own teachers, rooms and reviews.
  • Report numbers per branch, not only in total.

Pick 3–5 nurseries a parent would compare you with: same area, similar fees, similar curriculum or age range. Score each from 1 to 5. Score yourself honestly too.

Our voice and six building blocks

We sound like…

The teacher every parent hopes for: warm, calm, sure of what she's doing, and a little bit funny. Short sentences. No jargon. No shouting in capitals.

Say

"Every child settles in their own time. Here's how we help."

Not

"ADMISSIONS OPEN!!! LIMITED SEATS!!! BEST NURSERY!!!"

10:15 · The Craft Table

Which format for which job

Each format does one job best. Pick the job first, then the format.

Reel

Reach & feelings

15–30 seconds. Movement in the first 2 seconds. Subtitles always. Best for: a day in 60 seconds, activities, teacher moments, events.

Carousel

Saves & learning

5–8 slides, one idea per slide, the last slide asks "Save this". Best for: tips, checklists, explainers, settling-in plans.

Story

Daily warmth

No polish needed. Polls, quizzes, countdowns, the lunch menu, "today we…". Keeps you top of mind without filling the grid.

Photo

Clear messages

One strong picture, one message. Best for: review cards, open-morning invites, teacher of the week, announcements.

Live

Trust & expertise

Once a month, 20 minutes. Story time, ask the nurse, school-readiness Q&A. Save the recording to your profile.

Highlights

The mini website

Tour · Safety · Learning · Team · Reviews · FAQs · Admissions. Refresh covers once a term.

Pinned posts

The first impression

Pin 3 posts to the top: the tour reel, the safety reel and your best parent review. That's what new visitors see first.

Collab post

Borrowed reach

One post, shown on both accounts. Use it with partners: the dentist's tips, a bookshop's story time, a parent creator's visit.

When to post

Weekdays: 7–8am before drop-off, 1–2pm lunch break, 8:30–10pm after bedtime. Weekends: late morning. After 4 weeks, use your own Instagram Insights instead. Film during normal activities, never stage a lesson for the camera.

Stories, made easy

Polls ("Pasta or rice day?"), quizzes, a "today we…" photo, the lunch menu, countdowns to events. Stories don't need polish. They need to exist.

Weekends, sorted

On Thursday, schedule Saturday and Sunday posts in Meta Business Suite. Set a WhatsApp away message, and have admissions check messages twice a day (around 11am and 7pm). Weekend enquiries get a tour slot by Monday morning.

1:00 · Playdates

Influencers and partners

For a nursery, trust beats follower count. A local mum with 6,000 followers who lives five minutes away is worth more than a celebrity with 600,000 in another country.

Parent creators

Mums & dads who post

An honest tour, their first impressions, an open morning visit. Best if they have a child of nursery age.

Family lifestyle

Whole-family accounts

Weekend events, sports day, graduation-style moments. Warm, real, child-safe tone.

Micro & nano (1k–20k)

Parents near the branch

Best value by far. Their followers are your neighbours. Often happy with an experience rather than a big fee.

Language & community

Arabic and expat voices

Arabic-speaking creators for Emirati and Arab families; others for the communities your branch serves.

Experts

Child health & development

Paediatricians, paediatric dentists, speech therapists, nutritionists. Licensed only. Perfect for lives and workshops.

Educator creators

Early-years teachers online

Play ideas and learning tips. They lend credibility to your curriculum posts.

How to choose (the scorecard)

  • Permit first: a valid UAE Media Council advertiser permit. No permit, no deal.
  • Local audience: ask for a screenshot of their audience by city.
  • Real comments from real parents, not just emojis.
  • Child-safe content: respectful with their own children.
  • Values fit: nothing you'd be embarrassed to stand next to.
  • Not an advert wall: few paid posts, so their word still counts.

House rules for every visit

  • They film their own child only. Other children are never shown.
  • The nursery sees the content before it goes live.
  • Paid posts are clearly marked as ads or paid partnerships.
  • No fee claims or "best nursery" promises.
  • A short written agreement: what, when, how much, who approves.

Where to find them: who your current parents already follow, local parent groups, community pages, and the Media Council's list of approved agencies.

Partners worth a coffee

Shared audiences, zero ad spend. Each can become a workshop, a collab post or a giveaway that isn't "like & share".

1:30 · Pocket Money

Ads, on a sensible budget

Organic posts build trust. A small, steady ad budget makes sure local parents actually see them. Start small, measure cost per tour, then grow what works.

Starter

AED 1,000–2,000 / month

Boost the best 2 reels each month to parents within a few kilometres. One click-to-WhatsApp ad running all the time.

Growth · recommended

AED 2,500–4,500 / month

Starter, plus open-morning campaigns, retargeting people who watched your reels, and Google Search ads for "nursery + your area".

Peak months

+50% Jan–Mar, Aug

Lift spend when parents are deciding for September, and when new families arrive in late summer.

Targeting that works for nurseries

  • Location: a radius around the branch, plus nearby communities and office clusters.
  • People: adults likely to be parents of young children. Meta limits detailed targeting, so let good creative and location do the work.
  • Languages: English plus Arabic versions of the best ads. Add other languages your families speak.
  • Exclude current parents where possible, so the budget goes to new families.

Ad creative rules

  • Real teachers and real rooms beat stock photos every time.
  • First 2 seconds: movement and a face (a teacher's, not a child's, if consent is in doubt).
  • Subtitles on every video. Most people watch on mute.
  • One message per ad. One button. Ideally "Send WhatsApp message".
  • Prices and offers must match your regulator-approved fees.

2 · AskChild's age, start date, nearest branch

Getting reviews

Ask at happy moments: after the first parent meeting, after a great event, at graduation. Send the direct Google review link by WhatsApp. Never offer gifts in exchange for reviews.

Handling a bad review

Reply within 48 hours: thank them, say sorry for the experience, invite them to talk to the manager directly. No details about any child in public. Then fix the real issue.

Joining the conversation

Ten minutes a day commenting helpfully on local parent pages, community pages and partner accounts. Helpful, not salesy. A good tip is the best advert.

3:00 · Safe Hands Rules

Child safety and UAE rules

This is the section that protects the children, the families and the licence. Print it and stick it in the staff room.

Always

  • Get written photo consent at enrolment, with 3 clear options: public social media, private parent channels only, or no photos. Parents can change it anytime.
  • Keep a staff-only "photo list" per class so everyone knows who can appear.
  • Film on nursery-owned devices only. Upload to secure storage weekly, then delete from the phone.
  • Have a second person check every post with children before it goes live.
  • Check any paid influencer or creator holds a valid UAE Media Council advertiser permit.
  • Show award names and years exactly as given. Match all fee information to your regulator-approved fees.

Never

  • Children's full names, birthdays with names, or anything that identifies home or family.
  • Nappy changing, toileting, bathing, swimwear, sleeping faces or children upset.
  • Any detail about a child's health, behaviour or support needs.
  • Live location tags during trips or outings. Post afterwards.
  • Photos of children whose parents said no, even in the background. Blur or crop.
  • Staff personal phones or personal accounts posting nursery children.

Why this matters in the UAE

  • Posting someone's photo without consent can break Article 44 of the UAE Cybercrimes Law. Penalties include fines of AED 150,000 to 500,000.
  • Wadeema's Law (Federal Law No. 3 of 2016) gives every child a right to privacy. The PDPL generally requires consent to use personal data.
  • The Child Digital Safety Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 26 of 2025) came into force on 1 January 2026 and warns caregivers against harmful "sharenting".
  • Since 2025, people posting promotional content in the UAE need a Media Council advertiser permit. It's free, so ask to see it.
  • Your emirate's education regulator (for example KHDA in Dubai, ADEK in Abu Dhabi) sets fee and licensing rules. Check with them before any fee-linked offer.

This is a practical guide, not legal advice. Ask your regulator or a lawyer when unsure.
